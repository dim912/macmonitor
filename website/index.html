Open Source Software

Privacy Policy

MacMonitor is an open source application that respects your privacy:

  • No data is ever sent to remote servers
  • All monitoring is performed locally on your device
  • No personal information is collected
  • No analytics or tracking

The application only reads system metrics that are publicly available through macOS APIs.
